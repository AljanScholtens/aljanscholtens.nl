The Holten, near Vries in Drenthe, is the subject of my new photography project. I want to document this nature area over the years and eventually bring the images together in a photo book.

Returning to the same place changes how you look. The light shifts, seasons come and go, and the landscape changes too. By taking my time, I hope to notice and capture those small differences.

This project is just beginning. It is a collection that can grow slowly, with a book to work towards. I’ll share its progress here.

## A first taste: the zine

The book will combine photographs and stories. Before that, I’m making a zine with a first selection of photos. This is its introduction:

Wander the sandy paths of the Holten on a grey day and you might not see straight away what makes this small area special. But come back several times a week, always in different light, and you notice how the landscape slowly comes to you.

Walking the same paths again and again quiets your mind and gives your eyes room. Even after five years, I still find places I had been walking past at a few metres’ distance all along. It amazes me every time that something so close can go unnoticed for so long. As if nature reveals a little more of itself with every visit.

This zine is a first taste of a photo book in the making about the Holten near Vries. In it, landscapes and animals are joined by stories from people who know, manage and experience the area.
