The Holten, near Vries in Drenthe, is the subject of my new photography project. I want to document this nature area over the years and eventually bring the images together in a photo book.

Returning to the same place changes how you look. The light shifts, seasons come and go, and the landscape changes too. By taking my time, I hope to notice and capture those small differences.

This project is just beginning. It is a collection that can grow slowly, with a book to work towards. I’ll share its progress here.
